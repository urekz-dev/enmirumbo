# Changelog

All notable changes to this product are documented here. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Versions stay at 0.x until the cut-over, which is 1.0.0.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A local and dev panel page at `/admin/tareas`, for global `tasks.run`, to run one scheduled task as of a chosen date and locality time zone, preview its UTC instant and read counts or counts-only failures without running it again on reload. It works without JavaScript and is absent on stage and production.

- The retention purges, a task of the hourly job (`retention-purge`) that runs once a day for each locality, at 03:00 local time in its time zone (in every other hour it does nothing): rejected cards 90 local days after their rejection are deleted permanently exactly as the panel's permanent deletion does, photos first, with a `purge` entry of the scheduled job in the audit log; applied, discarded and superseded pending edits are deleted 30 local days after they were resolved, with any new photo no card names. The days are the locality's, in its time zone; a card sent again is not purged; a run does a bounded amount of work (20 cards, 20 edits, 15 photos) and the rest waits for the next day's run; an item that could not be purged stays whole, is counted and fails the run, with counts only. The purge of never-verified registrations waits for SMS verification, which is not built before the launch.
- A daily orphan-photo sweep in the hourly Worker job. It keeps card and pending-edit photos, waits seven days before deleting, resumes bounded passes next hour, fails the run with counts only when it could not sweep something, and purges the media hostname cache through the shared photo deletion path (EMR-57).
- Panel users and role bundles at `/admin/usuarios` and `/admin/roles`, with global permission checks, activation and assignment management, a concurrent last-super-admin guard, and an accessible panel home for roles without queue permissions (EMR-72).
- Direct rectification of any card from its panel detail, using the registration form and validation, with optional field and photo removal, locality permissions and atomic contact auditing. Published cards keep a local catalogue zone, with hidden zones available to the admin; cards in review or rejected keep the registration's free-text option. The panel can open WhatsApp with a copy of the card's captured data, trades and privacy notice acceptance, without internal review data or photo addresses.
- A Playwright audit of every panel screen at 390px with JavaScript disabled, using realistic names, zones, comments, reports, edits, photos, users and roles. It saves a screenshot per screen and checks overflow, main content and all tap targets.

### Changed

- Dev uploads and seed photos have their own `dev/photos/` folder in the shared media bucket, encoded by a `dev_` key marker. Copied stage photos still render on dev, while deletions, replacements, retention purges, cache retries and orphan sweeps touch only the environment's own folder; existing objects stay in place (EMR-75).

- Each deploy checks the catalogue URLs of its target database plus the pending migrations before applying them. The pending migrations run inside a transaction that is rolled back, so the check never changes the database, and a zone the panel added that would take a URL from a new category or trade stops the deploy (EMR-73).
- The panel navigation offers "Salir" through Cloudflare Access on shared phones. Checkbox and radio controls keep their normal 20px size inside label rows that meet the 44px touch target rule; public registration sizing is unchanged.

- Architecture decision records 0001–0018 and the product requirements document.

- ADR 0021: panel users, roles and permissions in the database.

- Three deployed environments, `dev`, `stage` and `prod`, on `workers.dev`, with their deploy workflows: `dev` by hand from any branch (its database is reset from `stage`, and the page footer names the branch and commit), `stage` on every push to `main`, and `prod` from a tag `vX.Y.Z`, which also creates the GitHub Release. Each deploy checks its build first and runs a smoke test after.

- Canonical and share-preview URLs are absolute and carry the full path of the locality, from the public URL of each environment.
- The tests fail when a link or a form of the site leads to a page that does not exist, when a link of a locality's page leaves out its state and municipality, or when a link that opens a new tab lacks `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

They belong to no locality and name none, and are prerendered and served as static files. The notice is at version 2: it names the processors (Cloudflare, Neon, Twilio, Resend and Umami) and links to Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum, says how the IP address is used, lists the two cookies, gives the retention periods and says that the rights are served from the panel. Until the legal review, both pages show their missing data in square brackets and a draft mark. Changing the text of the notice without raising its version makes the tests fail.
- With two or more active localities, `/` lists them, each with its municipality and state. Choosing one remembers it in a cookie that holds only its slug, for a year, and later visits to `/` go straight to it; the pages of a locality offer "Cambiar de municipio" to choose again. The list highlights the locality nearest to the visitor's approximate location, measured to its municipal seat, and never redirects by it. Activating a second locality is a data change only.

Phones no longer turn numbers in the text into call links. On `dev`, the branch and commit deployed now show in the footer of every page.

The deployed environments keep the closed one until the cut-over.

Each locality has its own image with the wordmark and its municipality and state, Tizayuca's first; a page outside a locality, or a locality without its image, shares the wordmark alone.

Until the launch sets the Website ID, no page loads it and no request leaves the site.

The panel keeps its stricter referrer policy. The deploy refuses a build whose static files lack them, and the smoke test checks them.

A card in review, rejected, deleted or of another locality answers the same 404 as one that never existed, a pending edit never shows, and a link with an earlier name redirects to the current one. Each card has its own title, description and share preview, declares itself to search engines as a local business with its zone but never its address or numbers, and is listed in the sitemap with its publication date. Only its WhatsApp button is measured.

They show only the published cards of the locality, newest first, 20 a page with "Ver más" to the next (`?pagina=2`), and offer only the zones that have cards. A trade page without published cards still answers, says so and asks search engines not to index it, and no page links to it. The trades on a card now link to their pages, and the sitemap lists the 8 listings and the trade pages with cards.

Its URL does not change.

With no results, or with nothing to search, the page offers the 8 categories; the notice of the empty search names the locality from its record. The page is never indexed, its title never carries what was typed, and an address that names no page answers 404.
- A per-IP quota on the search: 100 searches in 10 minutes per address, an IPv6 network counting as one. Over it, the page says "Recibimos muchas búsquedas desde aquí. Espera un rato y vuelve a intentar." and offers the categories, without running the search. The counters live only in the memory of a Durable Object, as codes made with a key that never leaves it, and each is deleted when its window ends: no address is stored anywhere. The registration form and the edit form count in the same Durable Object, each apart.
- The home of each locality, such as `/hidalgo/tizayuca`, replaces the notice that the directory is coming: "¿Qué necesitas en {municipio}?", the search box, which works without JavaScript, the 8 categories as large buttons and the "Deporte en {municipio}" block, which leads to the listing of sports clubs and schools. Every name comes from the locality record.
- Reporting a card: "Reportar este negocio", at the end of every card and apart from its contact buttons, opens a form at `/{estado}/{municipio}/negocio/<nombre>-<id>/reportar` with four reasons and an optional comment of up to 300 characters, without an account, without asking anything about the resident and without JavaScript. A report is confirmed at `/{estado}/{municipio}/reportar/gracias` with "¡Gracias por avisarnos! Vamos a revisar este negocio.", whether it was saved, caught by the invisible trap field or past the 10 unattended reports a card can gather. An error brings the form back with what the resident chose and wrote, read from the same submission: no cookie and nothing in the address. Each address can send 3 reports an hour, counted like the search and apart from it. A report changes nothing public and stores nothing about its sender. Neither page is indexed.
- "¿Es tu negocio?" with "Perdí mi enlace" at the end of every card: it opens WhatsApp to the contact number of the card's locality, with the message that asks for the management link already written.
- The photos of the cards, from a public media bucket of R2 served by Cloudflare without the Worker: the listing card shows a 400×400 photo, and only the first card of a list loads it at once; the card shows a 1200×900 one above its name, shares it as its preview on WhatsApp and Facebook, and names it in its data for search engines. Both are WebP, cached for a year. Only a photo key the server generated becomes an address, and only for a published card: no page, sitemap, preview or data block names the photo of a card in review, rejected or unpublished, and the media hostname lists nothing. Production has its own bucket, `enmirumbo-media` at `media-enmirumbo.urekz.com`; `dev` and `stage` share `enmirumbo-media-dev` at `media-dev-enmirumbo.urekz.com`. Locally the site serves its own bucket under `/_media/`. ADR 0011 is amended.

- Registering a business: each locality has its form at `/{estado}/{municipio}/registro`, on one screen and without an account, with the name, the category, the WhatsApp, the colonia and the consent required, and "¿Qué ofreces?", home delivery, landline, address, opening hours and Facebook optional. The example of "¿Qué ofreces?" follows the category. The colonia is typed with the zones of the locality as the browser's own suggestions ("hacien" offers "Haciendas de Tizayuca"); a name that is none of them is kept for the admin to normalize. The server checks every field, with the spec's messages next to each one, and keeps what was written when the form comes back, except the consent; the WhatsApp is saved as its ten digits, a number already in review or published is told so, and a rejected card resubmitted goes back to review. The consent block shows the simplified notice and the version being accepted, and the record stores the moment and the version in force, never one a submission sends; a notice that changed while the form was open asks to read it again. A registration is saved in review and lands on `/{estado}/{municipio}/registro/gracias` with "¡Gracias! Tu negocio está en revisión…". It works without JavaScript: the page's one script only changes the example and shows "Enviando..." while the form is sent. An invisible trap field and a quota of 3 registrations an hour per address, counted like the reports and apart from them, hold back bots; nothing about the sender is stored.
- "¿Tienes un negocio en {municipio}?" with "Registra tu negocio gratis" at the end of the home, and "Registra tu negocio gratis" in the empty states of a category, a trade and a trade in a zone, all to the registration page. The sitemap lists the registration page of every active locality.
- Cloudflare Turnstile on the registration form, in managed mode and in Spanish: the server checks each token with Cloudflare, sending only the token and the secret, never the visitor's address, and a submission without a valid token, or that Cloudflare cannot check within 3 seconds, saves nothing and comes back with "No pudimos comprobar que eres una persona. Vuelve a intentarlo." and what was written. Without JavaScript the page says "Para registrar tu negocio, activa JavaScript en tu navegador. Lo usamos para comprobar que eres una persona." and the form cannot be sent. Turnstile has a switch in the database, on by default, that an admin turns off with one SQL statement and no deploy (README, Operations); off, the form works without JavaScript and the trap field and the quota keep working. Local runs and the tests use Cloudflare's test keys without network, and no deployed environment accepts them. The site's content security policy needed no change. ADR 0012 is amended.
- The daily registrations alert: once a day, when a locality receives more registrations in its own day than its threshold (20 by default, changed with SQL), the hourly job writes an alert to the Worker log, with the locality and the counts only. It blocks nobody; the email to the admin arrives later.
- The first Worker secret, `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`, kept in the GitHub Environments and uploaded with each deploy; the deploy stops if it is missing, or if an environment's Turnstile site key is not a real one.
- The registration form takes one photo of the business, from the phone's gallery, with the photo policy above the field and "Dejar mi ficha sin foto" below it. The server takes only a real JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 5 MB and 40 megapixels, opened only after every other check, and turns it with Cloudflare Images into the listing card's 400×400 and the card's 1200×900, cropped from the centre, as WebP without any metadata (no location, no camera), stored under a new opaque key. Every other file, a photo too heavy or a server busy with other photos comes back next to the field with everything else that was written; when Images cannot process it, the form asks for another photo or to register without one. A rejected card resubmitted can change its photo or remove it, and the old photo's files are deleted; a registration that is not saved leaves no file behind.
- The edit mode of the management link: a published card's link, `/{estado}/{municipio}/editar/<token>`, opens the registration form prefilled with the card, headed "Edita tu ficha", without asking for consent again, and "Enviar cambios" saves the changes as a pending edit for the admin to review, while the card stays as it is on every public page. Sending changes again replaces the ones waiting, which the page warns about, and two submissions at once leave exactly one waiting. The same checks and messages as the registration, plus "Ese número ya está en otra ficha del directorio." for another card's number; Turnstile with its own action, a trap field and a quota of 3 edits an hour per address, apart from the others; and a new photo, or "Dejar mi ficha sin foto", which reach the card only when the edit is applied; a new photo already sent stays with later changes unless another is chosen, and the form says so. The confirmation is `/{estado}/{municipio}/editar/gracias`. The token is 256 random bits, of which the card keeps only a fingerprint; a link that opens nothing answers the same 404 as any other, and the address of the edit mode is never indexed, measured, cached or sent as a referrer. Links are issued by the panel, which arrives with epic F; nothing else issues one.
- The review screens of the admin panel, read-only for now. `/admin` is the review queue, "Registros por revisar": the cards in review and the pending edits of published cards, one row per card, from the one that has waited longest, each labelled "Alta nueva" or "Edición", with "Ya estaba publicada, la despublicaste" for a card the admin unpublished and "Lleva más de 48 horas" past the goal, counted above; and, below, "Negocios reportados", the cards with unattended reports. `/admin/negocios`, "Todos los negocios", lists every card, most recently registered first, 25 a page, with a status filter and a search by name or WhatsApp that work without JavaScript, and shows only name, zone, registration date and status. `/admin/registros/<id>` shows everything a card captured, its photo, its internal data, its unattended reports and "Escribirle por WhatsApp", and `/admin/ediciones/<n>` compares a pending edit with the published card, field by field and photo included. Each person sees only the localities and parts their roles allow; a screen with nothing to show answers 403, and a card of another locality 404. Dates are in the locality's time zone, and with two or more active localities the queue and the list filter by municipality. The panel shows the photo of any card it reviews from the media hostname; no public page names it. Locally, the panel's fixed identity needs a user with a role, like anyone (README).
- Approving and rejecting a registration from its detail in the panel, for whoever reviews registrations in its locality. "Aprobar y publicar" assigns up to 3 trades (or none), chooses a zone of the locality when the business wrote "Otra" (hidden zones included, marked "(oculta)"), marks whether it registered itself or was seeded, publishes the card and gives it its management link, and the confirmation, "Ya quedó publicado.", offers "Avisarle por WhatsApp" with the card's public link and the management link, warning "Mándale el mensaje ahora: este enlace solo se ve aquí.": the only time the panel shows a link, in the answer to the approval and nowhere else, never in an address, a cookie or the log. "Rechazar" needs a reason of up to 500 characters, never trimmed, stores it with its date, writes an entry of the audit log with the admin's email, and offers the notice with the reason. A decision applies only to a card still in review: of two at once exactly one wins, and the other says "Este registro ya lo habías resuelto." without issuing another link. A failed form comes back with the trades and the reason as they were. Approving an unpublished card publishes it again with its trades and a new link.
- A rejected card that its owner corrects and sends again is labelled "Reenvío" in the review queue, and its detail says "La rechazaste antes por: …" with the date: the resubmission keeps the rejection it answers.
- Attending reports and resolving pending edits in the panel. Each unattended report of a card's detail has "Marcar como atendido", for whoever attends reports in its locality: the detail answers "Reporte atendido.", the queue counts one fewer, and marking it again says "Este reporte ya lo habías atendido." without changing its date or anything of the card. The detail of a pending edit offers, to whoever resolves edits in its locality, "Aplicar los cambios", which copies exactly the fields of the form and the photo it asks for to the published card, asks for a zone of the locality when the owner wrote "Otra" (hidden zones included), refuses a number another card has, applies nothing to a card no longer published, deletes the photo the card stops naming and confirms with "Listo, la ficha ya se actualizó." and the notice with the card's link; and "Descartar los cambios", which needs a reason of up to 500 characters, never trimmed, leaves the card as it was, deletes the new photo the edit brought and offers the notice with the reason. An edit is resolved only once and only while it is the latest: otherwise the detail says "Estos cambios ya los habías resuelto." or "Estos cambios ya no son los últimos: el negocio mandó otros más nuevos.". None of them writes to the audit log.
- Unpublishing, a new management link and the permanent deletion, from the detail of a card in the panel, each for whoever holds its permission in the card's locality. "Despublicar" needs a reason of up to 500 characters, never trimmed, which the form warns goes to the business by WhatsApp; it puts the card back in the queue, marked "Ya estaba publicada, la despublicaste", keeps everything else of it, writes an entry of the audit log, and confirms with "Ya la despublicaste." and the notice with the reason. "Generar un enlace nuevo" makes the previous link answer 404 at once and shows the new one once, in the answer to its own submission, with "Mandarle el enlace por WhatsApp"; sending the same form again issues no other link. "Borrar definitivamente" leads to a confirmation of its own, where typing BORRAR deletes the card for good: its photos first, the card's and those of its edits, then the card with its trades, reports, edits and link in one transaction, with an entry of the audit log that outlives it. The screen that confirms it, "Ya se borró para siempre.", carries nothing of the card, and a deletion that cannot reach the photo store says the card was not deleted. Discarding an owner's changes is refused while the card is not published, since its notice tells the owner the card still is; they keep waiting until it is published again.
- The audit log of the panel, read-only, at `/admin/bitacora`: the destructive actions the panel already writes, newest first, 25 a page, each row with the moment in its locality's time zone, who did it (the Access email, or "Trabajo programado" for a purge), the action and the previous status in Spanish, and the card: its identifier as a link to its detail only while it exists and the user may open a card's detail in the entry's locality, by the detail page's own rule, and only its identifier, marked "Ficha borrada", once it was deleted, never a dead link and never a field of it. It needs `audit_log.view` in the entries' localities, so a user who holds it nowhere gets the 403; the queue and the permission-based panel home link to it as "Ver la bitácora" (a user whose only permission is `audit_log.view` lands on the home that leads to it), the locality filter appears with two or more, and the detail of a card shows a "Bitácora de esta ficha" section with its own entries. It reads only the table, never joins a deleted card's data, and offers no action, form or client JavaScript. The `edit_contact` entries of the admin's edits of contact data render with their Spanish label.
- Deleting a photo, whatever deletes it, also purges both of its addresses from Cloudflare's cache of the media hostname, where photos are kept a year, so a deleted photo stops being served. A purge that fails keeps only those addresses, and the hourly job retries them until they go; the permanent deletion then says the photo may still be seen a while. Two new Worker secrets, `MEDIA_ZONE_ID` and `MEDIA_CACHE_PURGE_TOKEN`, which the deploy requires; locally nothing is purged. ADR 0011, 0013 and 0020 are amended.
- Workers Logs and traces are off in every environment, written key by key in `wrangler.jsonc`, and the deploy refuses a build that would turn them on: an invocation log would keep the URL of every request, and the edit mode's URL is the key to a card. Debugging uses `wrangler tail`, which keeps nothing. ADR 0004 is amended.
- The zones screen of the panel, `/admin/colonias`, which the queue links to as "Administrar colonias": the zones of a locality, hidden ones included, by the name the site shows them with. The admin hides a zone, so the registration and the edit stop offering it, shows it again, renames it without changing its URLs, and adds one, such as a housing development that SEPOMEX does not list yet, whose URL comes from its name and never changes. A new zone is refused when another zone of the locality already has its URL, when it would take one of the site's own pages or make a URL read in two ways, and a new or renamed zone when two zones would be shown with the same name. Each change says it may take up to a minute to reach the site. It needs the permission to manage the zones of that locality, which now reads "Agregar, ocultar y renombrar colonias".

### Changed

- The registration form needs JavaScript while Turnstile is on, as ADR 0012 decided; with Turnstile off it still works without it.
- The colonia of the registration and edit forms is chosen from a list again, after the postal code: typing the postal code leaves in the list only the colonias of that code, and "Mi colonia no aparece", at the end, asks for its name, which is kept for the admin to normalize. Without JavaScript the list shows every colonia grouped by postal code. The postal code is required, is saved with the card and with its pending changes, and the panel shows it in the detail of a card and of its changes; no public page shows it. Each of the 105 zones of Tizayuca has its postal code from SEPOMEX's catalogue, the zones screen shows it, and a colonia added there needs one.
- The variable that names the hostname of the photos is `MEDIA_URL` (it was `PHOTOS_URL`), and the photo hostnames under `enmirumbo.com` are replaced, until that domain is on Cloudflare, by media hostnames under `urekz.com`. Cloud resources are named in English, with hostnames one level below their zone.
- The brand is written enmirumbo, lowercase and in one word.
- The smoke test after each deploy follows the redirect of the home page, which leads to the locality while only one is active.
- The database keeps only the reference data of Tizayuca, the one city the directory serves: the state of Hidalgo, the municipality of Tizayuca, its locality and its 105 zones. The other 31 states and 2,477 municipalities of INEGI's catalogue are removed from every environment; their addresses answer 404, as before. Cities are added one at a time, each in its own data migration. ADR 0006 is amended.
- Locally, `/robots.txt` is the open one that production will serve after the cut-over.
- The quota counter is one single Durable Object for every purpose, instead of one per purpose: each purpose still counts apart, with the same limits, answers and privacy. Written rules, checked by tests, make a runaway bill impossible: nothing in its storage, no alarm, a timer only while it counts and never more than 10 minutes ahead, and no preview URLs. At most one object is ever awake, inside what Workers Free and Workers Paid include. ADR 0012 is amended.

### Removed

- The short address of a locality, such as `/tizayuca`, which only `dev` and `stage` served: public addresses always carry the state and the municipality, and `/tizayuca` answers 404. ADR 0007 is amended.
- The scripts that wrote the migrations of INEGI's catalogue and of SEPOMEX's zones (`vp run db:inegi` and `vp run db:zones`). How the data of a new city is prepared is decided when one is needed.
